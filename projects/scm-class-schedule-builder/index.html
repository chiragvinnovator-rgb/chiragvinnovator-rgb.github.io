SCM Class Schedule Builder

A shared tool for building a department's course schedule

For
The Supply Chain Management department, W. P. Carey School of Business
Type
Faculty-sponsored project
When
2026
Team
Team of 4

Overview

One schedule that everyone works from

A web tool for building, reviewing and managing a department's course schedule. It was built by a team of four under the direction of a professor, outside regular coursework.

Need

Many constraints, one schedule

A term's schedule has to match courses, instructors, rooms and time slots, avoid clashes, and respect what instructors have asked for. When that is done by hand, conflicts tend to be found late and preferences are hard to weigh fairly.

Requirements

What the tool had to do

  • Show one live schedule that everyone sees
  • Limit editing to administrators and leave viewing open
  • Flag conflicts while the schedule is being built, not afterwards
  • Show how well each instructor's assignments match their preferences

Solution

What was built

  • A schedule table built by dragging courses, instructors, rooms and times into place
  • Automatic checks for double-bookings and room clashes, with softer warnings for preference mismatches
  • A score from 0 to 100 percent for each instructor's match to their preferences, with a one-click optimiser
  • Sign-in with a university email, and separate administrator and view-only access
  • Changes published to every signed-in user at once

Status

A pilot, not yet adopted

The tool is being piloted. The Supply Chain Management department will adopt it if the pilot succeeds.

This is a pilot under evaluation, not an implemented department system.

My contribution

Access design and clearer views

I contributed to the access design that separates people who can view the schedule from those who can change it, and to clearer viewing, filtering and controlled update workflows.
